# Desk-Rules-v1
Responsible-use rules for the Event Support Desk.

**Owner:** Khawla Alomari · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 2026_10_15

Each rule says who, what and when, and can be answered with yes or no.

| # | Area | Rule | Yes / no test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data | Before pasting an inquiry, the agent replaces personal information with codes such as User-1 or Request-X. | Did I remove personal information before pasting? |
| 2 | Verification | Before sending, the agent checks that all event information matches the Desk Facts, including opening hours and the 48-hour ticket transfer rule. | Does the reply match the Desk Facts? |
| 3 | Fairness | Before a template enters the library, its owner runs the swap test and records any unexplained differences. | Is the swap-test result recorded? |
| 4 | Policy | Before sending, the agent refers refund status inquiries to the finance team and never requests ID numbers, passwords or bank details. | Did the reply follow the refund and data safety rules? |
| 5 | Human decision | Before sending, a human reviewer checks the AI-generated reply for accuracy and compliance with the event policies. | Did a person review the reply before sending? |
